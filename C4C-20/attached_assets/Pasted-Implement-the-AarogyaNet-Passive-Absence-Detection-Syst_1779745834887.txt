Implement the AarogyaNet Passive Absence Detection System.

GOAL:
Treat missed visits, unavailable patients, and broken continuity as healthcare intelligence signals instead of missing data.

This is a key part of AarogyaNet's "Clinical Memory as Infrastructure" philosophy.

==================================================
1. VISIT CONTINUITY TRACKING
==================================================

Track:
- expected visit frequency
- actual completed visits
- missed visit streaks
- patient unavailable events

Support:
- chronic disease monitoring continuity

==================================================
2. ABSENCE SIGNAL DETECTION
==================================================

Generate alerts for:
- repeated missed visits
- sudden disappearance after consistent follow-up
- adherence interruption
- high-risk patients becoming unavailable

Examples:
- "Patient unavailable 3 of last 5 scheduled visits"
- "Previously compliant patient missed first visit in 6 months"

==================================================
3. LONGITUDINAL MEMORY INTEGRATION
==================================================

Absence events should appear in:
- patient timeline
- doctor longitudinal summaries
- clinical alerts
- risk escalation summaries

==================================================
4. RISK ESCALATION
==================================================

Absence should influence risk.

Examples:
- repeated absence → raise longitudinal risk
- high-risk patient unavailable → escalation flag

Keep deterministic and interpretable.

==================================================
5. OFFLINE-FIRST SUPPORT
==================================================

Ensure:
- unavailable visits sync correctly
- timestamps preserved
- continuity calculations remain valid offline

==================================================
6. DOCTOR VISIBILITY
==================================================

Doctors should see:
- continuity breakdown
- absence trends
- disappearance alerts
- visit regularity metrics

==================================================
7. DASHBOARD POLISH
==================================================

Add:
- continuity badges
- adherence continuity graphs
- absence warning cards

==================================================
8. PRESERVE ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

Do NOT redesign:
- GCPE
- longitudinal engine
- visit system

Extend existing continuity intelligence cleanly.

==================================================
OUTPUT
==================================================

The system should now behave like:
"a healthcare continuity intelligence layer."
