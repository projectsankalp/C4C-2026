Why AarogyaNet Uses Blockchain

JUDGES' REFERENCE
Immutable ASHA Visit Verification
Every ASHA worker visit is hashed and sealed into a block. The clinical data — BP, symptoms, medications, risk score — cannot be altered after recording. This creates an unbreakable audit trail for India's community health programme.
Anti-Fraud Healthcare Auditing
Ghost visits, falsified records, and data manipulation are cryptographically impossible. Each block's hash references the previous block — any modification breaks the entire chain, instantly surfacing fraud attempts to supervisors.
Tamper-Proof Prescription Trail
Prescriptions issued through AarogyaNet are fingerprinted and sealed with ECDSA digital signatures. Any modification — dosage, medication name, date — invalidates the cryptographic signature, providing court-admissible evidence of tampering.
National Health Infrastructure Integrity
AarogyaNet's SHA-256 + ECDSA + Proof-of-Work blockchain implements the same cryptographic standards used in financial audit systems — adapted for rural healthcare delivery, where paper records are easily lost or falsified.
