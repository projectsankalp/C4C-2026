# Digital Detox / Digital Wellbeing Notes

## Why Digital Detox Matters

* Phones are useful but can sometimes make people feel distracted or disconnected from real-life
interactions.

* Balance between digital and offline life improves:

* Sleep

* Mental health

* Academic performance

* Relationships

* Focus and productivity

* 77% of University of Arizona undergraduate students felt the need to reduce phone usage.

---

# What Is Digital Detox?

Digital detox means:

* Reflecting on your relationship with your phone

* Becoming mindful of how and why you use devices

* Taking intentional steps to reduce unhealthy usage

---

# Two Key Themes of Digital Detox

## 1. Feelings While Using Your Phone

Things to observe:

* Enjoyment level

* Feelings of guilt/regret

* Mental relaxation vs overstimulation

* Feeling connected/disconnected

* Mood changes

* Feeling emotionally triggered

* Your intentions while using the phone

---

## 2. Time Spent on Phone

Things to track:

* Daily average screen time

* Peak usage hours

* Most-used apps

Helpful tools:

* iPhone Screen Time

* Android Digital Wellbeing

# Declutter Your Digital Space

## Organize Apps

Ways to organize:

* Prioritize useful apps

* Move distracting apps away from home screen

* Group apps into folders

---

## Uninstall Apps

Reasons to remove apps:

* Too distracting

* No longer useful

* Unused apps

* Trigger stress/comparison

* Privacy/storage/battery concerns

---

# Curate Your Social Media Experience

## Positive Social Media Habits

* Follow positive/inspiring accounts

* Unfollow stressful or negative accounts

* Use “hide” or “snooze” features

* Take social media breaks

* Detox together with a friend

---

# Finding Balance in Daily Life

## During Social Situations

Suggestions:

* Keep phone in pocket/bag

* Put phone in another room

* Use phone baskets

* Use phones for memories, not distractions

Try limiting phone use during:

* Meals

* Classes

* Meetings

* Conversations

* Nature moments

Avoid checking phone while:

* Driving

* Reading

* Relaxing

* Exercising

* Using the bathroom

---

# Sleep and Phones

## Helpful Habits

* Listen to calming sounds

* Use bedtime stories

* Read books in night mode

* Listen to audiobooks

* Use sleep tracking apps

* Reduce bedtime screen use

## Habits to Reduce

* Doomscrolling

* Endless TikTok/YouTube watching

* Shopping at night

* Reading stressful news

* Celebrity gossip consumption

Bonus:

* Move phone farther away

* Use traditional alarm clock
