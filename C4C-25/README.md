GreenPath is a Node.js web application that helps users find sustainable routes and understand local environmental conditions. Users can detect their current location, choose nearby rural destinations, view AQI-based environmental insights, and receive health and community safety recommendations.

The app supports English, Kannada, and Hindi with talkback voice guidance for accessibility. It also includes offline support, low-connectivity messaging, sensor status indicators, community alerts, and sustainable route redirection through Google Maps. GreenPath integrates with the Awaaz grievance reporting platform so users can report pollution, unsafe roads, garbage burning, smoke, or other environmental hazards directly from the AQI dashboard.

Together, GreenPath and Awaaz create a citizen-focused environmental awareness and reporting platform for rural and low-connectivity communities.
